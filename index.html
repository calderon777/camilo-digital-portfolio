Profile

Economics with an operator's mindset.

I am an applied economist and academic leader with a PhD in Financial Economics. At City St George's, University of London, I teach macroeconomics and monetary economics, direct the student experience for the Department of Economics, and serve as Digital Champion for the School of Policy & Global Affairs.

My work connects macroeconomic modelling, financial analysis and digital tools to practical decisions—from reproducible cyber-impact analysis to AI-assisted assessment with honest evaluation. I enjoy ambiguous problems: defining the question, building a defensible evidence base, creating the workflow and communicating what decision-makers should do next.
